Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for speed and simplicity on your local network.

🔒

100% Private

Files never leave your local network. No accounts, no telemetry, no surprise uploads to someone's server.

⚡

Full WiFi Speed

No bottleneck through distant servers. Transfer at the full speed your local network supports — often 100 MB/s+.

📲

No App on Phone

The desktop app runs a local web server. Any phone or tablet on the same WiFi can send files through its browser.

📦

Any File, Any Size

No 2 GB cap, no compression, no format restrictions. Send a 4K video or a full project folder without splitting it.

🖥️

Cross-Platform Desktop

Native apps for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Lightweight, no Electron, instant startup.

🛜

Automatic Discovery

Devices on the same WiFi automatically find each other via mDNS. No IP addresses to type or remember.

How it works

Three steps. That's it.

No setup wizards, no pairing codes, no cloud accounts.

1

Launch StayLocal

Open the app on your Mac, Windows, or Linux machine. It starts a local server instantly.

2

Connect from any device

On the same WiFi, open a browser and go to the address shown in the app — or scan the QR code.

3

Drop & receive

Drag files into the browser window. They arrive on your desktop in seconds. Done.

Your files never leave your home.

Other "quick transfer" tools quietly route your files through their servers. StayLocal is different — the transfer path goes directly from your phone to your computer over your own WiFi. We can't see your files because they never reach us.
